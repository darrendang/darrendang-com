# DarrenDang.com — Book 4 Public Companion Posture

**Status:** PUBLISHED / IMPLEMENTED

**Public implementation:** DarrenDang.com  
**Canonical authority:** private `darrendang/dang-genome`

## Decision

DarrenDang.com may publicly acknowledge **The Making of The Way: How Conversation Becomes Discovery** as a **companion work in development** while preserving **The Path Forward** as the bounded three-book trilogy:

1. _For Those Who Come After Us_ — INHERIT
2. _Wisdom Has No Rank_ — BECOME
3. _D.O.T.S._ — CREATE

The Book 4 cover may carry its approved series/book-number design language, while the website architecture explicitly explains that the work is a companion rather than a fourth movement in the trilogy.

## Public posture

The governing posture is:

> **Signal, don’t sell.**

Book 4 may have a visible title, approved cover, restrained Books-page presence, dedicated detail page, and a bridge to Generative Dialogue. It should not receive a homepage takeover, preorder treatment, countdown, launch banner, or public release-date commitment at this stage.

Book 1 remains the foreground publication priority.

## Generative Dialogue

The public page is synchronized around the plain-language thesis:

> **Generative Dialogue is disciplined conversation that changes what becomes possible next.**

The page presents the recurring movement as recursive rather than procedural:

> **Question → Conversation → Reflection → Challenge → Refinement → Discovery → Practice → Pass Forward**

_The Making of The Way_ is presented as the primary longitudinal case study behind the public idea.

## Book-status language

Use evergreen public labels:

- Book 1 — **Preparing for Publication**
- Book 2 — **In Final Editorial Preparation**
- Book 3 — **In Advanced Development**
- Book 4 — **In Development**

Remove public publication-date promises unless Darren intentionally reauthorizes a date commitment.

## Navigation and restraint

The primary navigation remains unchanged:

> **The Way | Explore | Books | Ideas | About**

Book 4 and Generative Dialogue remain inside the site architecture rather than being promoted into top-level navigation.

## Cover provenance

The Book 4 website asset is a resized/compressed derivative of the cover artwork explicitly approved by Darren for public website use on 2026-09-04. It is a production derivative, not a generative redraw or reinterpretation of the approved art.

## Public/private boundary

This decision publishes only the approved public title, subtitle, cover, high-level thesis, relationship to the trilogy, and Generative Dialogue framing. It does not publish private manuscript content, private provenance records, unpublished source conversations, internal Dang OS governance, or any additional material from the private Dang Genome.
