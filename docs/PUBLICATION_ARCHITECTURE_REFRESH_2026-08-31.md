# DarrenDang.com Publication Architecture Refresh — 2026-08-31

**Status:** PUBLIC IMPLEMENTATION CHANGE  
**Approved by:** Darren Dang  
**Date:** 2026-08-31

## Purpose

Refresh DarrenDang.com so the public experience reflects the current expression architecture of The Way without turning the site into a content catalog.

Primary navigation remains reader-centered:

**The Way · Explore · Your Way · The Way Forward · About**

Publication streams are made legible beneath **Explore** rather than promoted into top-level navigation.

## Implemented Changes

1. **Explore** now distinguishes four expressions of The Way:
   - Letters from Darren — lived reflection;
   - Books / The Path Forward — preserved wisdom;
   - The Way Papers — living ideas;
   - The Way in Motion — demonstrated practice.
2. Added a dedicated **The Way in Motion** landing page. No unpublished case is exposed.
3. Reframed **Papers & Talks** so The Way Papers and applied professional papers remain distinct.
4. Updated **Letters from Darren** to reflect the preserved twelve-Letter opening collection and its three arcs without exposing unpublished manuscripts or titles.
5. Added a restrained homepage bridge to The Way Papers and The Way in Motion.
6. Synchronized public D.O.T.S. language around **Deconstruct → Observe → Transfer → Synthesize → Create**, while preserving **Collect Dots. Connect Dots. Create Something New.** explicitly as origin-line lineage.
7. Added The Way in Motion to the footer and sitemap.

## Explicit Non-Changes

- The Way Letters are not exposed publicly.
- No unpublished LFD-001–012 manuscript is published.
- No unpublished Way Paper is published or previewed by title.
- No unpublished Way in Motion case is published or previewed by title.
- Primary navigation is unchanged.
- The public site remains quiet, literary, reader-centered, and not media-heavy.

## Publication Boundary

This implementation exposes **stream architecture**, not unpublished work.

The governing preview rule is:

> **Preview the stream, not the unpublished artifact.**

Each future Letter, Way Paper, or Motion case still requires its own public-release approval.
