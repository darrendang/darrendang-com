# DarrenDang.com — ADR-0024 Experience Architecture Alignment

**Status:** PUBLISHED / IMPLEMENTED / CURRENT ALIGNMENT  
**Date:** 2026-08-29  
**Public implementation:** DarrenDang.com  
**Private canonical authority:** `darrendang/dang-genome`  
**Governing private decision:** ADR-0024 — The Way Experience Architecture 1.0

## Controlling public model

DarrenDang.com remains an intentional public expression of The Way and a governed public projection of the Dang Genome.

The philosophical spine is:

> **The Way → Your Way → The Way Forward → Pass Forward**

`Pass Forward` is an optional act of stewardship, not a fourth constitutional layer and not a content-acquisition funnel.

## Canonical practice layer

The site now exposes the Five Movements as the public practice layer:

> **Receive → Examine → Connect → Practice → Pass Forward**

They are recursive questions and feedback loops, not commandments or a completion checklist.

Public route:

> `/use-the-way/`

Your Way is not a sixth movement. It is the evolving lived expression that emerges through experience and repeated practice. The Way Forward remains actor-owned direction.

## Human Journey boundary

The public site may show:

> **Curiosity → Connection → Discovery → Transformation → Contribution → Stewardship**

only as a possible relationship/development map.

It must not be represented as a required sequence, loyalty ladder, maturity score, conversion funnel, or persistent user classification.

## Public experience rules

The site follows these ADR-0024 rules:

1. Human question before system.
2. Story and lived voice before architecture dump.
3. Invitation before instruction.
4. Progressive depth rather than requiring the visitor to understand the Genome first.
5. Multiple legitimate entry points.
6. Darren is an example and founding steward, not the destination.
7. Questions may organize exploration across content formats.
8. Major deep pages should explain what the artifact is, how it connects to The Way, and where a visitor can go next.
9. D.O.T.S. is one signature method within The Way, especially useful inside Connect; it is not The Way itself.
10. The Dang Genome is the first living family implementation of The Way, not the only possible implementation.

## Primary navigation

Primary navigation remains:

> **The Way | Explore | Your Way | The Way Forward | About**

`Use The Way` is intentionally available as a practice route without adding another top-level primary-navigation burden.

## Contribution production gate

The earlier rich public Shared Wisdom intake is **paused**.

The contribution concept remains public at `/contribute/`, but `/share/` no longer exposes a submission form and is removed from the sitemap.

The private review infrastructure is preserved for future reuse.

Rich public intake must not reopen until the production gate required by Contribution Architecture 1.0 is satisfied:

1. Contribution Permission Schema;
2. Contribution Review Protocol;
3. Minor / Vulnerable Contributor Policy;
4. Acceptance & Safety Policy;
5. Public Contribution UX and notices;
6. Correction / Representation Challenge / Withdrawal workflow;
7. enforceable storage and AI-analysis controls;
8. appropriate legal review.

Contribution, canonization, and publication remain separate states.

## Public knowledge-graph behavior

Connected Dots remains the preferred near-term public graph behavior: expose meaningful, explainable relationships before adding graph visualization or graph-database machinery.

Connected Dots now hands readers toward the Five Movements and Your Way rather than routinely nudging contribution.

## Content hierarchy

- **The Way** — philosophical root and starting point.
- **Use The Way** — Five Movements practice layer.
- **Your Way** — evolving lived expression owned by the person.
- **The Way Forward** — actor-owned direction.
- **Pass Forward** — optional stewardship/contribution.
- **The Path Forward** — Darren's trilogy within The Way.
- **Letters from Darren** — lived learning in motion.
- **D.O.T.S.** — one signature method and way of seeing problems.
- **Dang Genome** — first living family implementation and connected body of governed knowledge.
- **Dang OS** — private governance and operating layer.
- **DarrenDang.com** — governed public experience, not canonical source of truth.

## Publication and privacy boundary

This alignment changes the public website only. It does not publish private Genome material, living-person material without permission, unpublished IP beyond already approved public expression, or internal Dang OS governance details that are not intentionally public-safe.

The private repository `darrendang/dang-genome` remains the source of truth for canonical philosophy, architecture, provenance, privacy, and publication authority.

## Change rule

Future visual, editorial, and feature changes may evolve when compatible with ADR-0024. Material changes to human-agency boundaries, practice semantics, contribution governance, public/private projection rules, primary navigation logic, or the role of the Genome/D.O.T.S./AI require deliberate reconciliation against the private canonical source.
