# DarrenDang.com — Canonical Alignment Lock

**Status:** PUBLISHED / IMPLEMENTED / LOCKED

**Public implementation:** DarrenDang.com  
**Private canonical authority:** `darrendang/dang-genome`

## Controlling public model

DarrenDang.com is the intentional public expression of The Way and must remain aligned to the constitutional relationship:

> **The Way → Your Way → The Way Forward**

The public experience may continue with an optional contribution movement:

> **The Way → Your Way → The Way Forward → Pass It Forward**

`Pass It Forward` is an action, not a fourth constitutional layer.

## Protected distinctions

1. **The Way** is the portable philosophical root and stronger starting point.
2. **Your Way** is the reader's evolving lived expression. No one's Way is the final Way.
3. **The Way Forward** is the direction the reader chooses as inherited wisdom meets present reality.
4. **Pass It Forward** is the later, optional act of preserving and contributing learning for others.
5. **The Path Forward** is Darren's three-book trilogy within The Way; it is not another name for The Way Forward.
6. **The Dang Genome** is the living body of knowledge and relationships beneath the public experience.
7. **Dang OS** remains private governance and operating infrastructure.
8. **D.O.T.S.** remains a method and way of seeing problems, not the philosophical root.

## Public navigation lock

Primary navigation follows:

> **The Way | Explore | Your Way | The Way Forward | About**

The public homepage is reader-journey-first rather than content-container-first.

## Public knowledge-graph behavior

The public graph should be experienced before it is visualized. Explicit, explainable related-object navigation is preferred over premature graph infrastructure.

Core content should increasingly expose meaningful connections among Letters, Ideas, Stories, Books, D.O.T.S., Papers/Talks, Shared Wisdom, and later corrections or alternate perspectives.

A `For Your Way` reflection handoff may help readers test material in their own context without prescribing their Way.

## Shared Wisdom consent boundary

Initial contribution intake authorizes private review only. Public release requires a later explicit approval of the proposed public version.

> **Submit → Private Review → Select → Edit → Contributor Approves → Publish**

Submission, publication, and Darren canon remain separate states.
