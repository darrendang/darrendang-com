## 2. Public-repository boundary

`darrendang/darrendang-com` is public.

- private family records or living-person material not approved for publication;
- unpublished manuscripts or private source documents;
- confidential OCERS/employment information;
- credentials, secrets, tokens, or security-sensitive configuration;
- private membership/authorization data;
- restricted Drive artifacts;
