# DarrenDang.com Navigation Refinement — 2026-09-20

**Status:** CANONICAL / CURRENT / PUBLIC IMPLEMENTATION CONTROL  
**Approved by:** Darren Dang  
**Approval date:** 2026-09-20  
**Scope:** primary navigation and homepage discoverability within the approved books-first reader journey

## Decision

Darren reviewed the current DarrenDang.com reader journey and explicitly approved a surgical navigation refinement rather than a website redesign.

The approved reader journey remains:

> **Lived experience → stories and books → recurring patterns become visible → The Way emerges → Your Way / The Way Forward → deeper ecosystem**

The September 14 books-first architecture remains governing.

## Primary navigation

The approved primary navigation is:

> **Books · The Way · Letters · Ideas · Genome · Explore**

### Rationale

- **Books** remain the primary public doorway.
- **The Way** remains the larger pattern that emerged through lived experience and the books.
- **Letters** are elevated because they now carry substantial living-legacy and ongoing-learning material, including Bố Ơi, Mẹ Ơi, Bố Mẹ, and The Way Was Us.
- **Ideas** remain a direct route into durable concepts emerging from the work.
- **Genome** remains available as the connected body beneath the public expressions, but does not precede Books or The Way.
- **Explore** remains the broad discovery route.
- **Papers & Talks** move out of scarce primary-navigation space and remain available through Explore, the homepage deeper-ecosystem section, and the footer.

## Hero hierarchy

The hero continues to privilege exactly two primary actions:

1. **Begin with the books**
2. **Discover The Way**

A restrained tertiary line may expose:

> **Letters · Ideas · Genome**

These are secondary discovery links, not equal-weight hero calls to action.

The purpose is to improve information scent without weakening the books-first hierarchy.

## Homepage deeper-ecosystem section

The existing "Go deeper" section remains after the human/books/Way/Letters journey.

Its destinations should be explicitly clickable:

- Ideas
- Papers & Talks
- The Dang Genome
- Shared Wisdom

This improves discoverability without moving deeper-system material ahead of the reader's human entry path.

## Responsive behavior

Responsive navigation should preserve the hierarchy rather than hide links arbitrarily.

Preferred compression order:
- preserve **Books**, **The Way**, **Letters**, and **Explore** longer;
- allow **Ideas** and **Genome** to collapse earlier on narrower screens;
- on small mobile layouts, the existing Explore CTA may remain the compact route to the full ecosystem.

## Boundary

This decision does **not**:
- reorder the homepage's fundamental reader journey;
- move Genome or system architecture ahead of Books/The Way;
- restore a system-first homepage;
- add a new top-level conceptual layer;
- change the five-book progression.

It is an approved navigation/discoverability refinement inside the September 14 baseline.

## Governing principle

> **The books remain the doorway. The Way explains the pattern. Letters show the learning still alive. Ideas and Genome provide depth without becoming the first burden placed on the reader.**
