## Relationship to Bố Ơi

This song is **not** an English translation or adaptation of the Vietnamese song `Bố Ơi`.

The two works have different emotional centers:

- **Bố Ơi** — Vietnamese memory, sacrifice, refugee/family history, service, education, and the twenty-five-year accountability promise.
- **I Miss You Differently Now** — the grown son recognizing that he understands his father more deeply after becoming a father himself.

The central English-song realization is:

> missing his father after twenty-five years is not simply more or less grief; it is a deeper grief shaped by understanding.

This publication addendum does not replace or rewrite the existing Bố Ơi memorial canon.
