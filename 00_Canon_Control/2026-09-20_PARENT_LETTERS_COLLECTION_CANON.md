## Approved interpretive distinction

The three works have distinct roles and should not be collapsed into one generic memorial:

- **Bố Ơi** — accountability, inheritance, sacrifice, education, service, and the promise carried forward.
- **Mẹ Ơi** — care, devotion, resilience, `Lễ · Nghĩa · Nhân · Tín · Chí`, and intergenerational continuity through Phi Tâm.
- **Bố Mẹ** — the marriage itself and the inheritance created by a shared life across hardship, family formation, and time.

The joint letter is a capstone, not a replacement for either individual letter.
