## Lyric experience

For every non-English song:
- the original/native lyric is the primary creative expression;
- the English companion is displayed beside it for understanding;
- the pair is collapsed by default behind an explicit expandable control;
- opening the control presents a side-by-side native-language / English-companion reading on larger screens and a stacked reading on smaller screens.

For the English / United States song, the expandable panel contains the original English lyric without a duplicate translation.

The English companion is explanatory/stewardship text, not automatically a singable English adaptation and not the controlling source lyric.

## Audio behavior

- no autoplay;
- one native browser audio player per song;
- audio appears before optional lyrics;
- the reader may listen in sequence or jump to a language;
- all MP3 bytes are governed by `docs/AUDIO_REVIEW_MANIFEST.json`;
- musical works are artistic interpretations and do not replace the Constitution or language/cultural authority.
