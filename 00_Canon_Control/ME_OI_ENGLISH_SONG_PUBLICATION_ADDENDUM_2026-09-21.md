## Relationship to Mẹ Ơi

This song is **not** an English translation or adaptation of the Vietnamese song `Mẹ Ơi`.

The two works have different emotional centers:

- **Mẹ Ơi** — Vietnamese memory, childhood care, Bố Mẹ's marriage, `Lễ · Nghĩa · Nhân · Tín · Chí`, and intergenerational continuity through Phi Tâm.
- **Mom, I Know Now** — the grown son recognizing that much of what felt ordinary in childhood was quiet sacrifice, and understanding more of what his mother carried only after becoming a parent himself.

The central English-song realization is that a child can receive love without understanding its cost, then recognize that cost years later in the way he loves his own children.

This publication addendum does not replace or rewrite the existing **Mẹ Ơi** letter, 2004 source letter, Vietnamese song, or parent-letter publication control.
