# DarrenDang.com Website Baseline Governance

Date: 2026-09-14
Status: CANONICAL
Approved by: Darren Dang
Scope: Public website structure, reader journey, and redesign governance

## Canonical baseline

The current DarrenDang.com reader journey and site structure are the approved baseline.

**Lived experience → stories and books → recurring patterns become visible → The Way emerges → Your Way / The Way Forward → deeper ecosystem**

The current site should be treated as the default structure going forward unless a materially better website redesign is proposed and explicitly approved by Darren.

## Structural-change guardrail

Do not materially restructure, re-sequence, or redesign the website without Darren's explicit approval.

Before proposing any structural change, provide Darren with:

1. the specific problem in the current baseline that the change is intended to solve;
2. evidence or reasoning showing why the current structure is insufficient;
3. the proposed alternative structure;
4. the expected reader-experience benefit;
5. tradeoffs, risks, and what would be lost or weakened;
6. whether the change is incremental and reversible or constitutes a material redesign;
7. a clear recommendation: keep the baseline or approve the redesign.

No material structural change should be implemented merely because a new interview, idea, book update, or individual piece of feedback suggests an interesting addition. New evidence should first be tested against the existing baseline.

## Default decision rule

If the current website can absorb a new idea through copy refinement, content addition, deeper-page updates, or minor presentation changes without disturbing the reader journey, preserve the baseline.

Restructure only when the redesign creates a demonstrably better reader experience or resolves a material problem that cannot be solved cleanly within the current architecture.

## Baseline characteristics to preserve

- Human story and lived experience are the first doorway.
- The books are the primary public journey and precede abstract system explanation.
- All five books are visible as a widening reader journey.
- The Way is introduced as the larger pattern that became visible through lived experience and the books.
- Book 3 is human-first before method-first.
- Book 4 is the making story before the epistemology.
- Book 5 is the handoff to the reader rather than publicly framed through internal architecture terminology.
- Deeper governance, ontology, Dang Genome, Dang OS, Papers, methods, and constitutional material remain accessible without crowding the first-time reader experience.
- The master principle remains: each generation should inherit a stronger starting point, not a narrower future.

## Change classes

### Does not require redesign approval

Routine maintenance that preserves structure, including:
- typo or grammar corrections;
- stale-link repair;
- factual/status updates;
- accessibility improvements;
- responsive/layout fixes that do not alter information hierarchy;
- visual consistency fixes;
- book-status updates;
- copy refinements that preserve the established reader journey;
- security, dependency, build, or deployment maintenance.

### Requires Darren's explicit approval before implementation

Material changes including:
- homepage section reordering that changes the reader journey;
- changing the primary narrative hierarchy;
- changing the role or relative prominence of Books, The Way, Your Way, The Way Forward, Genome, or other major layers;
- adding/removing major top-level navigation concepts;
- changing the five-book public progression;
- converting the site from human/books-first back to system-first or another substantially different model;
- major visual redesigns that alter how the site is understood, not merely how it is styled.
