Status: CANONICAL + PUBLISHED + PERMANENT RETENTION

## Purpose
This record establishes a permanent-retention rule for DarrenDang.com family-letter artifacts that Darren has explicitly approved for public publication and preservation.

These artifacts may move within future information architecture, navigation, taxonomy, design systems, or URL structures, but they must remain preserved and publicly accessible somewhere on DarrenDang.com unless Darren explicitly revokes this instruction.

A website redesign or reorganization is not, by itself, permission to remove, archive off-site, unpublish, or silently replace a protected artifact.

## Retention rule
The content, provenance, identity, and approved public-media relationships of these artifacts are permanent elements of the Dang Genome and DarrenDang.com public legacy layer.

Future website changes may:
- change navigation placement;
- change visual presentation;
- change taxonomy or section names;
- change routes if redirects are preserved;
- improve accessibility, metadata, responsive behavior, or technical implementation.

Future website changes must not:
- delete a protected artifact;
- remove public access without Darren's explicit approval;
- drop a companion audio asset where one is part of the artifact without an explicit replacement decision;
- rewrite historical or personal source material merely to fit a new website architecture;
- break old inbound links without redirects when routes change;
- collapse the works into generic biography or marketing content that loses their identity and provenance.

For the Chrystina artifact specifically, ordinary redesign or cleanup must not silently discard either the featured v2 arrangement or the original recording. The known incomplete ending of the original recording is preserved as publication history rather than hidden or treated as permission to remove it.

## Reorganization guardrail
Before any future site restructuring that affects Letters, family material, The Way, Book 1, or the public legacy layer, the implementation review must explicitly verify all protected artifacts still exist and remain reachable.

Navigation is flexible. Preservation is not.

## Dang Genome meaning
These works are not disposable campaign content. They are living-legacy artifacts showing inheritance across relationships and generations:

- Chrystina artifact: partnership, family formation, shared life, and the lived foundation beneath later ideas.
- Bố artifact: inheritance, accountability, sacrifice, education, service, stewardship, and passing forward what was received.
- Mẹ artifact: care, devotion, resilience, relational duty, the five family words, and intergenerational continuity through Phi Tâm.
- Bố Mẹ artifact: marriage as a shared source of inheritance and a family starting point created across a lifetime together.

Together they demonstrate that The Way grows out of lived relationships before it becomes language, framework, or publication.

## Authority
This rule is subordinate only to Darren's explicit future instruction to revise or revoke it. Ordinary refactoring, redesign, cleanup, archival work, or navigation simplification does not override it.
