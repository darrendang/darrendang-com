# Permanent Web Artifacts — Family Letters

Status: CANONICAL + PUBLISHED + PERMANENT RETENTION
Approved by Darren Dang: 2026-09-16

## Purpose
This record establishes a permanent-retention rule for two family letters and their companion songs on DarrenDang.com and within the Dang Genome.

These artifacts may move within future information architecture, navigation, taxonomy, design systems, or URL structures, but they must remain preserved and publicly accessible somewhere on DarrenDang.com unless Darren explicitly revokes this instruction.

A website redesign or reorganization is not, by itself, permission to remove, archive off-site, unpublish, or silently replace either artifact.

## Protected artifacts

### 1. To Chrystina — The Way Was Us
- Current route: `/letters/the-way-was-us/`
- Companion song: **The Way Was Us**
- Current audio: `/public/audio/Song-You-Are-My-Way1.mp3`
- Meaning: a birthday letter and song to Chrystina preserving the lived family foundation behind much of what Darren later articulated as The Way.

### 2. Bố Ơi — Twenty-Five Years Later
- Current route: `/letters/bo-oi/`
- Companion song: **Bố Ơi**
- Current audio: `/public/audio/Song-bo-oi.mp3`
- Meaning: a 25-year accountability letter to Đặng Văn Phi, paired with the Vietnamese memorial song and the preserved 2001 `Con Nhớ Bố` source letter.

## Retention rule
The content, provenance, and identity of both artifacts are permanent elements of the Dang Genome and DarrenDang.com public legacy layer.

Future website changes may:
- change navigation placement;
- change visual presentation;
- change taxonomy or section names;
- change routes if redirects are preserved;
- improve accessibility, metadata, responsive behavior, or technical implementation.

Future website changes must not:
- delete either artifact;
- remove public access without Darren's explicit approval;
- drop the companion audio without an explicit replacement decision;
- rewrite the historical or personal content merely to fit a new website architecture;
- break old inbound links without redirects when routes change;
- collapse the two works into generic biography or marketing content that loses their identity and provenance.

## Reorganization guardrail
Before any future site restructuring that affects Letters, family material, The Way, Book 1, or the public legacy layer, the implementation review must explicitly verify both protected artifacts still exist and remain reachable.

Navigation is flexible. Preservation is not.

## Dang Genome meaning
These two works are not disposable campaign content. They are primary living-legacy artifacts showing inheritance across relationships and generations:

- Chrystina artifact: partnership, family formation, shared life, and the lived foundation beneath later ideas.
- Bố artifact: inheritance, accountability, sacrifice, education, service, stewardship, and passing forward what was received.

Together they demonstrate that The Way grows out of lived relationships before it becomes language, framework, or publication.

## Authority
This rule is subordinate only to Darren's explicit future instruction to revise or revoke it. Ordinary refactoring, redesign, cleanup, archival work, or navigation simplification does not override it.
