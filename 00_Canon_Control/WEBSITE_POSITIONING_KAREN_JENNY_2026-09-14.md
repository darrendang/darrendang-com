# Website Positioning — Karen + Jenny Reader-Response Synthesis

Date: 2026-09-14
Status: CANONICAL
Approved by: Darren Dang
Sources: September 13, 2026 Darren–Dr. Karen Hill Scott conversations; September 14, 2026 Darren–Jenny Sadosky reader-response conversation
Scope: Public website positioning and reader journey

## Canonical positioning correction

The website should let the books and human story earn The Way rather than present The Way as a finished system that explains the books.

Default first-time reader journey:

**Lived experience → stories and books → recurring patterns become visible → The Way emerges → the reader's own life / Your Way / The Way Forward → deeper ecosystem**

This is a positioning and sequencing rule, not a change to the underlying philosophical hierarchy or governance architecture.

## Why

Karen and Jenny independently encountered the work through people, stories, relationships, memory, vulnerability, purpose, and recognition before abstract frameworks.

Karen's reader-level synthesis of the first three books was effectively:

1. what comes to us through family and what came before;
2. what we learn from other people;
3. what we make from those first two sources.

Jenny described the transferable power of the books as personal stories that invite readers to recognize their own lives, reflect, and eventually tell or pass forward their own story.

The website should preserve that discovery path rather than front-load ontology, taxonomy, or internal architecture labels.

## Public positioning rules

### 1. Books are the primary doorway

The homepage should lead with why the writing began and present all five books as the clearest reader journey.

### 2. The Way is discovered after the books

Publicly, The Way should be introduced as the larger pattern that became visible across the books and lived experience. The books are not merely illustrations of a prewritten system.

### 3. Human language before internal architecture language

Prefer reader questions such as:

- What has life taught me, and what is worth passing forward?
- Who else might have something to teach me?
- What can I make from everything I have learned?
- What happens when a human and AI think together over time?
- What will I do with what I inherit, learn, and discover?

Internal labels such as `learning across source`, `learning across domain`, `collaboration / provenance emerging`, and `integration layer` may remain useful in governance and manuscript architecture, but should not dominate the public front door.

### 4. Book 3 remains human before methodological

D.O.T.S. should first answer the human question of what we can create from what we have learned. The more rigorous method—Deconstruct, Observe, Transfer, Synthesize; search-space expansion; CREATE; recursive learning—remains available underneath.

### 5. Book 4 is a making story before an epistemology

The Making of The Way should first invite the reader into how the books and philosophy were actually made: memory, relationships, old experiences, new dialogue, corrections, failures, and human-AI collaboration. Generative Dialogue and provenance remain central, but they should be discovered through the story rather than used as the opening sales language.

### 6. Book 5 is a handoff to the reader

The Way Forward remains the integration work internally, but public positioning should focus on the reader-facing question: **What will you do with all of this?** Avoid leading with the phrase `integration layer`.

### 7. Deeper architecture moves deeper

The Way Constitution, Dang Genome, Dang OS, Papers, methods, governance, and formal ontology remain important. They should be available after the human and book-level doorway rather than crowding the first-time homepage experience.

## Guardrails

- Do not weaken or rewrite The Way philosophy merely to make the site simpler.
- Do not erase provenance, corrections, reader-response evidence, or governance distinctions.
- Do not convert intimate grief or family material into marketing copy.
- Do not imply that AI authored Darren's lived experience or replaces human judgment.
- Preserve the master principle: each generation should inherit a stronger starting point, not a narrower future.
- Preserve GitHub as the current-state authority for the website.

## Implementation direction approved

- Reorganize homepage around human origin → five books → The Way → wider ecosystem.
- Put `Books` before `The Way` in primary navigation.
- Reposition the Books index around five reader questions rather than internal system labels.
- Simplify the public doorway into Book 3 while retaining rigorous method underneath.
- Humanize Book 4 around the making of the work.
- Reframe Book 5 as the handoff to the reader and remove `integration layer` as dominant public language.
- Unwind the September 14 Jenny surgical website implementation while retaining its findings as provenance.
